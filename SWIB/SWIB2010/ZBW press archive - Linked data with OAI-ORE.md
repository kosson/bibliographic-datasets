# ZBW press archive: Linked data with OAI-ORE
[[Joachim Neubert]]

The press archive of the [[ZBW]] contains around 6 million articles. Up until 2005, folders with structured content on people, companies, subjects and goods were kept there. In order to make the digitized holdings accessible and citable under unique addresses, they are currently being processed as Linked Open Data. The [[OAI-ORE]] standard is used, which enables the addressing and description of deeply nested aggregations. The project makes it clear how central problems from the library and [[cultural heritage]] world can be solved with [[ORE]]. The W3C standard [[RDFa]] allowed the straightforward creation of a [[REST]]-based web application. Approaches to overcoming performance and availability bottlenecks will be presented in the lecture. At the same time, it is demonstrated how existing resources can be enriched with data from the [[LOD]] Cloud, e.g. by integrating abstracts from Wikipedia into the display via PND and [[DBpedia]].
